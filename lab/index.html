Section (all members)

Defaults ≈ W310x39. Verify section properties with the CISC handbook.

1 · Stiffness

Each member gets a 6×6 Euler–Bernoulli matrix (EA/L, 12EI/L³, 6EI/L², 4EI/L, 2EI/L), rotated with k = Tᵀk′T and assembled into K. Truss members keep only axial terms.

2 · Consistent loads

Point loads anywhere on a member and partial or trapezoidal distributed loads become equivalent nodal loads f = ∫Nᵀq dx with the element shape functions (4-point Gauss), giving exact nodal displacements.

3 · Cases and combinations

K·u = F is solved once per load case (D, L, S, W). Results are linear, so each combination is a weighted sum. The envelope is the max and min over all ULS combinations.

4 · Recovery

End forces f = k′Tu − f_eq, then N, V and M by statics along each member, reactions R = Ku − F, and the deflected shape by integrating M/EI twice between the nodal displacements.

Combinations follow the pattern of NBCC Table 4.1.3.2-A; verify factors against the current edition. Linear elastic, small displacements, one section for all members, no self-weight, P-delta, buckling or resistance checks. Distributed loads are per metre of member length; gravity acts down, lateral acts in +x. Moments are counter-clockwise positive; moment diagrams are drawn on the tension side. For learning and preliminary checks, not a substitute for design by a P.Eng.
